Pirates of Perdition

What Is Perdition?

Perdition — a state of ruin, destruction, or being forever lost.

An old word with a dark meaning, perdition speaks of the place beyond safety — where the familiar world ends and uncertainty begins.

For sailors, that boundary has always been the sea.

Why Pirates of Perdition?

Because we sail where land ends.

The Strait of Georgia can be beautiful, calm and welcoming — and it can quickly remind you who is really in command. Wind, waves, current and weather have ruled these waters far longer than any captain.

Pirates of Perdition is a nod to the old seafaring idea of venturing beyond the safety of shore and into the unknown.

We aren't pirates headed for perdition.
We're the pirates who know the way through it.
